# Memory Through Practice

**A free REF learning mini-workbook | October 2026 | Reader edition 1.0**
**Audience:** Secondary and university students
**Learning outcome:** Use independent recall, correction and changed examples to test learning.

## How to use this book
Read the short explanation, attempt its practice before checking the worked answer, then change the example and repeat from memory.

## 1. Rereading is not recall

Close the page and explain the idea in your own words. Familiar text can still be hard to retrieve independently.

**Your practice:** Without looking, explain evaporation in one sentence.

**Worked example / answer check:** Liquid water can change to water vapour; compare your account with a trusted explanation.

**Transfer:** Try a new case from a different topic, without copying the example; write down what you checked.

## 2. Make feedback specific

Instead of merely writing “wrong,” identify the exact mistaken term, step or assumption.

**Your practice:** A learner says all gases are invisible. What is the useful correction?

**Worked example / answer check:** Find a visible coloured gas example and distinguish a gas from smoke or droplets.

**Transfer:** Try a new case from a different topic, without copying the example; write down what you checked.

## 3. Revisit after a gap

Return to questions after time passes and try without your notes. Record which answers you actually retrieved.

**Your practice:** Plan three short reviews for four vocabulary items.

**Worked example / answer check:** Try now, tomorrow and a few days later, with the last questions mixed and no notes.

**Transfer:** Try a new case from a different topic, without copying the example; write down what you checked.

## 4. Transfer to another context

Change numbers or situations so you practise a rule rather than memorising an answer.

**Your practice:** After 20% of 50, create and solve another percentage question.

**Worked example / answer check:** 15% of 80 is 12; explain by 0.15 × 80.

**Transfer:** Try a new case from a different topic, without copying the example; write down what you checked.

## Independent challenge
Track one concept through closed-notes explanation, feedback, later retrieval and a new application.

Use this evidence card: **before / action / expected / observed / one limitation / next test**.

## Five-day practice
- Day 1: Try lesson one before reading its example answer.
- Day 2: Recall the core idea with notes closed; check and correct.
- Day 3: Try another lesson with a changed example.
- Day 4: Complete the independent challenge.
- Day 5: Show the result to a suitable reviewer and revise one step based on real feedback.
