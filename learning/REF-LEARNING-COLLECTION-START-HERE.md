# REF Learning Collection — 10 Free Mini-Workbooks

A collection of short, practical learning guides derived from generalizable REF/Logistinfra lessons. Each guide has four lessons with exercises and example answers, one independent challenge, and a five-day practice routine.

**Edition:** October 2026. Educational impact has not been independently evaluated.

## Choose a learning goal

1. **[One Small Step](./01-one-small-step.md)** — Young learners, 9–12 (with adult or teacher guidance). Turn a large goal into one safe five-minute action and visible check.
2. **[Evidence Detective](./02-evidence-detective.md)** — Students aged 10–14. Tell apart observation, hypothesis and unanswered question.
3. **[Debug Like a Detective](./03-debug-like-a-detective.md)** — Beginning programmers and students, 13+. Describe, reproduce, isolate and retest a small error.
4. **[Memory Through Practice](./04-memory-through-practice.md)** — Secondary and university students. Use independent recall, correction and changed examples to test learning.
5. **[Use AI, Check Your Work](./05-use-ai-check-your-work.md)** — Teenagers and first-time AI users, 14+. Ask for help while preserving independent verification and privacy.
6. **[Finish a Stuck Project](./06-finish-a-stuck-project.md)** — Older students and independent builders, 15+. Recover the latest real project state and complete one bounded next action.
7. **[Turn Notes into a Useful Guide](./07-turn-notes-into-a-guide.md)** — Student writers, educators and creators, 15+. Convert raw notes into one safe, teachable resource with an independent task.
8. **[Show Real Proof](./08-show-real-proof.md)** — Students and first-job applicants, 16+. Create a source-linked portfolio case study without inflated achievements.
9. **[Test a Real Problem](./09-test-a-real-problem.md)** — Student entrepreneurs, freelancers and new operators, 16+. Verify a workflow pain and try the lightest safe intervention with a willing user.
10. **[Teach for Independent Use](./10-teach-for-independent-use.md)** — Peer tutors, teachers, mentors and educational creators. Design a brief lesson that ends with an unaided transfer task.

## How to use
Pick just one guide that fits your current need, attempt the task without looking at the solution, and record what happened. Educators can use the practice prompts with age-appropriate supervision. These are free reader editions; detailed PDF and Markdown editions are available as conversation attachments in the original authoring session.

## Report one correction
When giving feedback, include the guide title, exercise number, what you tried, whether you completed it independently, and which instruction was unclear. Do not include private learner or client information.
