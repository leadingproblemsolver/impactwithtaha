# Small Steps & Evidence Detectives — Free Activity Sampler

**Ages 9–12 · Parent/teacher-guided printable learning activity · REF Learning Collection · October 2026**

This free sample adapts two missions from the longer *Small Steps & Evidence Detectives* printable workbook. It encourages children to break down difficult tasks and check facts before jumping to conclusions. It has not been independently evaluated for learning outcomes.

## Mission 1 — One small step

Big goal: **“I will get better at science.”** That does not tell us where to start.

Try: **“I will draw and label three parts of a plant in five minutes.”**

**Your turn:** Write one goal you have been postponing. Turn it into one safe action you can begin in five minutes.

- Big goal: ___________________
- First action: ___________________
- Evidence that I finished: ___________________

## Mission 2 — Clue or guess?

Circle the observation and underline the explanation in each pair:

1. **“The library door is locked.”** / **“The library is closed all day.”**
2. **“The pencil box is empty.”** / **“Someone stole the pencils.”**
3. **“The worksheet has a missing page.”** / **“The printer skipped a page.”**

**Your turn:** Make your own observation and a possible explanation. How would you check the explanation safely?

## Answer check (for adults and learners)

In all three pairs, the first statement is observable. The second is a hypothesis that needs a check. A strong response to Mission 1 names a physical action and a verifiable result. Ask a learner to try a different example without hints before concluding they understood.

## Parent and teacher notes

Demonstrate one example, ask a related question, then remove your hints for a different example. Invite the learner to say *what is known*, *what is guessed* and *what check would help*. Keep feedback anonymous and avoid collecting children's identifying information.
