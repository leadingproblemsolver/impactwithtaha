What is actually causing it?

I separate the visible symptom from plausible causes before anyone buys another tool or adds headcount.

What should be tested first?

One bounded change with a clear measurement. If the evidence is weak, the answer can be “do not change this yet.”

Illustrative output

A decision the client can act on, without pretending we know more than we do.

Observed symptom

Paid leads are arriving, but customers often wait for an estimate before they can book.

First uncertain handoff

Qualified request → estimate ready for the customer.

Possible causes

Owner approval, missing job details, supplier lookup, unclear pricing rules, or simple queue delay.

Evidence to collect

10 recent leads: arrival time, qualification complete, estimate ready, booked/not booked, and who touched the quote.

Smallest test

Measure quote latency first. If one repeatable lookup or rule dominates the delay, prototype only that step.

Do not change yet

Do not increase lead volume, replace the CRM, or automate the whole workflow before the delay is verified.

Illustrative example, not a client-result claim. The real diagnostic is built from the client’s actual workflow and evidence.
