# Claim Registry — Public Safe Boundary

## Global rules
- AI assistance is explicit. Do not imply unaided authorship where the work was AI-assisted.
- Separate repository contents from independently demonstrated human competence.
- Offline tests prove deterministic/local contracts only.
- Deployment readiness is not production verification.
- Architecture is not user value.
- Outreach/distribution infrastructure is not revenue or adoption.
- No invented performance, adoption, client, revenue, or ROI metrics.

## Safe public claims

### Multi-repo engineering hardening — SUPPORTED
Safe: "Hardened and verified multiple AI-assisted repositories across state integrity, contracts, security boundaries, tests, CI, and deployment readiness."
Do not say: "Productionized six client systems" or "prevented production incidents."

### Five-system packaged release — OBSERVED
Safe: "Released five executable systems with 50 automated tests, five installable wheels, five CLI entry points, and five primary smoke workflows."
Do not say: "Used in production" or "adopted by customers."

### DriftGuard — OBSERVED/SUPPORTED
Safe: "Built a full-stack AI decision-boundary application with deterministic constraint precedence and bounded semantic AI interpretation."
Safe metric: "52 release checks + 5 judgment tests in the six-repo validation report."
Additional recorded benchmark: "24/24 agreement, zero critical false passes, 17 unsupported passes prevented."
Do not say: "Production-proven" or "improved ROI."

### SignalOps — OBSERVED
Safe: "Implemented deterministic relevance ranking, permission-aware escalation, durable state, and CRM-ready export boundaries."
Safe metric: "11 unit tests + smoke + compile passed offline."
Do not say: "Live CRM integration" unless separately verified.

### Market Intelligence Pipeline — OBSERVED
Safe: "Implemented provider-neutral ingestion, strict normalization, retries, provenance, deduplication, source isolation, and deterministic exports."
Safe metric: "13 tests + smoke + compile passed offline."
Do not say: "Live provider reliability" or "automated real-world scraping" unless separately verified.

### TraceCrumb — OBSERVED/SUPPORTED
Safe: "Built an incident-triage application with fallback behavior, Supabase boundaries, telemetry surfaces, and a tested incident-memory graph."
Safe metric: "32 static checks + 45 graph/domain tests."
Do not say: "Reduced MTTR" or "improved first-action success."

### Chat-to-Post — OBSERVED
Safe: "Built an evidence-grounded LLM workflow with structured outputs, evidence classification, adversarial gates, and human approval."
Safe metric: "9 tests + 20/20 fixture validation in the packaged release."
Do not say: "Autonomous publishing" or "proven engagement lift."

## Current highest-value missing proof
1. Paid client requirement.
2. Client-authorized live deployment/integration.
3. Client-verified result.
4. Public review/testimonial.
5. Repeat engagement or quantified business outcome.
