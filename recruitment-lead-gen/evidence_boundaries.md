# Evidence Boundaries

## OBSERVED / SOURCE-BACKED

- A real Freelancer recruitment-lead-generation brief exists.
- A 10-row buyer-specific campaign sample was prepared on 2026-08-29.
- The private buyer pack contains company size snapshot, HR/TA/People owner, enriched business email, professional route, source URL, personalization evidence, 3-touch copy and execution fields for each row.
- Current tracker state is 10 prepared prospects / 0 sends / 0 replies / 0 meetings.
- Historical seed state is 15 companies / 20 emails / 0 replies.
- Public careers/hiring sources were rechecked on 2026-08-30 where marked in `sample_targets.csv`.

## INFERRED / SYSTEM-GENERATED

- Priority A/B.
- Whether a public hiring/growth signal creates a useful recruitment-services opening.
- Whether a specific source fact is the strongest personalization angle.
- Whether a role is likely to own external recruitment decisions.
- Whether the new multichannel sequence is better than the historical seed approach.

These are hypotheses, not outcomes.

## UNKNOWN

- buyer acceptance;
- deliverability;
- target-company appetite for external recruiting support;
- reply rate;
- meeting rate;
- placements;
- revenue;
- causal effect of trigger gating, owner resolution or multichannel sequencing.

## PROHIBITED PUBLIC CLAIMS UNTIL RECEIPT EXISTS

Do not say:
- “campaign succeeded”;
- “generated candidates”;
- “booked meetings”;
- “improved hiring”;
- “increased reply rate”;
- “produced pipeline/revenue”;
- “GDPR compliant” as a blanket legal conclusion;
- any ROI, placement or hiring-outcome claim.

## GDPR / privacy boundary

The workflow uses minimization, role-relevant B2B context, provenance, clear sender identity, opt-out/suppression and human approval as operating safeguards. That is **GDPR-conscious workflow design**, not legal certification or a substitute for the client's legal/process review.

Public repository samples omit personal business emails and person-level profile URLs even when those exist in the private buyer handoff.
