# Kill / Narrow Conditions

Do not scale this campaign merely because the machinery works.

## Stop or narrow when

1. **Desk fit is unresolved.** The client has not confirmed specialisms, role families and geographies.
2. **Source freshness fails.** A target no longer passes the size, owner, contact or trigger gate immediately before launch.
3. **Contact quality is weak.** The owner is generic/irrelevant or the route is guessed rather than enriched/resolved.
4. **Personalization is unsupported.** Copy requires an inferred pain that is not grounded in a public source.
5. **Deliverability fails.** Bounces or sender-domain issues dominate before message relevance can be evaluated.
6. **No reply-quality signal emerges.** After the agreed calibration tranche, do not simply increase volume; compare target class, owner class, trigger and message.
7. **Correction burden dominates.** If the client repeatedly has to fix account selection or owner mapping, the qualification logic is not yet saving work.
8. **Buyer rejects the operating shape.** If the buyer does not value the tracker/handoff cadence, do not keep polishing it internally.

## Next irreversible receipt

Preferred order:
`buyer correction/acceptance → approved launch → delivery receipt → substantive reply/non-response → qualified meeting → economic outcome`

Every upgrade must be attributable to a real external event.
