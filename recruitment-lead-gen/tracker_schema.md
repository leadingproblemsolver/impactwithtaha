- Industry

- LinkedIn route

- Contact/source status
- Touch 1 subject

- Touch 3 follow-up
- Prospects added
- Emails sent
- Social touches
- Replies
- Qualified meetings
- Status
- Next action

- what converted;
- what did not;
- best-performing trigger;
- buyer language / objections;
- next-week allocation.

No manual rewriting of poor results into success language.
