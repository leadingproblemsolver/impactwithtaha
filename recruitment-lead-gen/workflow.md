# Workflow

```text
LIVE BUYER BRIEF
→ extract hard constraints
→ generate candidate companies
→ exact 100–999 employee gate
→ resolve accountable HR/TA/People owner
→ enrich business contact route
→ attach current public trigger + source
→ classify PASS / HOLD / EXCLUDE
→ generate one-evidence / one-CTA 3-touch sequence
→ client confirms desk / specialism / geography fit
→ revalidate source + contact
→ launch approved tranche
→ record sends / social touches / replies / meetings
→ Friday report
→ scale, narrow, or kill based on reply-quality evidence
```

## Human approval boundary

The system may propose accounts, evidence and copy. It does not autonomously authorize outreach.

Client/operator approval remains required for:
- recruitment desk and specialism fit;
- geography;
- sender identity/domain;
- suppression list;
- final source/contact freshness;
- launch.

## Failure behavior

- Unknown headcount → exclude until resolved.
- Weak owner → do not substitute a random employee.
- Missing email → preserve missing state; never guess.
- Stale trigger → hold/research.
- Unsupported personalization → remove it.
- No replies after the agreed sample → do not scale volume by default; inspect targeting/message/deliverability first.
