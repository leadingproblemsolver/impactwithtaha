# Qualification Logic

| Gate | PASS | FAIL | Required evidence | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Company size | 100–999 employees in the enrichment snapshot | <100, >999, or unresolved | source/enrichment-backed headcount band | keep only exact-band accounts |
| Buyer ownership | HR Director, TA leader, People leader, or relevant departmental HR owner | generic employee or irrelevant function | current professional title + resolvable route | one accountable owner per account |
| Reachability | business email enrichment and/or usable professional route | no usable route | enrichment result + profile/source reference | never invent an address |
| Commercial trigger | current hiring, growth, expansion, employer-brand, or role-volume evidence | stale/no relevant trigger | public company/careers/news source | downgrade or hold weak rows |
| Personalization | specific fact tied to public source | generic compliment or inferred pain | evidence URL + concise source-backed statement | mention only supported fact |
| Sequence | bounded email → social → email | blast / unlimited chase | approved copy with one CTA | launch after client desk/specialism fit |
| Measurement | prospects, sends, social touches, replies, qualified meetings | opens-only reporting | shared tracker | Friday report from same ledger |

## Freshness rule

The August 29 sample is a **snapshot, not an evergreen list**. Headcount, title, email and public trigger are revalidated immediately before launch.

## Ownership rule

A company passing the size gate does not pass the campaign gate until an accountable recruitment/People owner and a plausible contact route exist.

## Trigger rule

Public hiring evidence is a reason to inspect relevance, **not proof that the company buys external recruitment support**.
